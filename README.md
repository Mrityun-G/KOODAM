KOODAM is a full-stack service marketplace application designed to connect customers with service providers through a centralized digital platform. The application simplifies the process of discovering services, connecting with professionals, managing service requests, and tracking service activities.

The platform aims to bridge the gap between customers looking for reliable services and service providers seeking opportunities to showcase their skills and reach more customers.

KOODAM provides a structured ecosystem where users can register, explore available services, submit service requests, and communicate with service providers. Service providers can manage their profiles, list their services, handle customer requests, and monitor their service history.

The application is designed with a modular architecture that supports customer management, service provider operations, administrative control, and intelligent service processing.

By combining modern web technologies, database integration, and machine learning capabilities, KOODAM aims to deliver a scalable and user-friendly service marketplace that improves accessibility, transparency, and efficiency in local service management.
